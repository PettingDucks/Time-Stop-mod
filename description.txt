Adds Orion's Half, a pocket watch that stops time for everything except players.

While time is stopped:
- Enemies, bosses, town NPCs and critters freeze in place and cannot hurt you
- Enemy projectiles hang in the air and cannot hurt you
- Your own weapons, minions, building, mining and placing all work normally, so you can attack frozen enemies
- The clock, weather, events, liquids, grass growth, falling sand, dropped items, scenery and enemy spawns are paused

Hits on frozen enemies are stored and land all at once when time resumes; frozen bosses take reduced damage. Use it again to resume time. Time also resumes on its own after a limit (10 seconds by default), followed by a recharge. Both are changeable in the mod config.

With Calamity: crafted after the Devourer of Gods from a Gold or Platinum Watch, 12 Cosmilite Bars, 10 Darksun Fragments, 10 Nightmare Fuel, 10 Endothermic Energy, 4 Ascendant Spirit Essence and 3 Twisting Nether at a Cosmic Anvil. Without Calamity it is a post-Moon Lord item.

With Wrath of the Gods: there is one being that time does not apply to.
